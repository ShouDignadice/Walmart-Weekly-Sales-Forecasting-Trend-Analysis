## Trend Analysis


Trend analysis was performed to understand Walmart weekly sales behavior before building forecasting models. After preparing the `Date` column and creating time-based features, sales were analyzed by week, month, holiday period, and store.

The analysis showed that sales peak during November and December, with December having the highest average weekly sales at approximately \$1.28M. January had the lowest average weekly sales at approximately \$924K, suggesting a post-holiday decline. Holiday weeks also showed higher average sales than non-holiday weeks, and the top sales weeks were largely concentrated around Black Friday and Christmas.

Store-level analysis showed that average weekly sales vary significantly across locations. Overall, the findings suggest that Walmart sales are influenced by seasonality, holiday demand, and store-level differences, which supports the use of time series forecasting models such as ARIMA and SARIMA.
